# Design Job Board Feed

Open internships, co-ops and entry-level roles for Industrial Design, Graphic Design and UI/UX students in the University of Illinois School of Art & Design. Updated each weekday.

- `postings.csv` and `postings.json`: open postings only.
- Columns: Program, Title, Company, Type, Term, Location, Region, Pay, Apply by, Found, Note, Link.

Listings are gathered automatically and can close without notice. Confirm details on the employer's site before applying.

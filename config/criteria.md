# What counts as a qualifying job

These rules decide which postings go on the ID + GD Job Board. The daily routine (`ROUTINE.md`) follows them, and so should anyone adding a posting by hand.

## Scope

Many good roles are not titled "industrial designer" or "graphic designer". Count all of these when design is the main job.

**ID (Industrial Design):** 3D designer, CAD designer/3D modeler, packaging designer (structural), CMF (color, materials, finish), exhibit/environment design, footwear design, furniture design, soft goods (bags, apparel accessories, outdoor textiles), hard goods, home goods/housewares, kitchen and bath, retail design (store design, fixtures, point-of-purchase displays, visual merchandising), toys and juvenile products, sporting goods/outdoor/fitness equipment, transportation design (automotive, vehicle interiors, aviation, marine, powersports), medical device design and human factors design, lighting design, jewelry and accessories, eyewear and watches, model making/prototyping, clay and digital sculpting, design research, sustainability/circular design.

**GD (Graphic Design):** web designer (visual/front-of-site design, not development), brand designer, visual designer, communications designer, marketing designer, creative/design roles in marketing teams, art direction (junior/associate art director), apparel graphics, promotional products and merchandise graphics, packaging graphics, motion design, video graphics and editing for design teams, animation, illustration, editorial/publication design, book and magazine layout, type design, environmental and experiential graphics, wayfinding and signage, event and trade-show graphics, production artist, prepress and print production, presentation/pitch deck design, social media design.

**UX (UI/UX & Digital Product):** UX, UI, interaction, digital product design, "Product Designer" at software companies.

**Strict rule:** design must be the main job. Skip marketing/communications coordinator, social media manager or merchandising roles where design is a side task, and skip engineering roles (mechanical, design, prototype, packaging engineering) even if titled "design".

## Qualify

Add a posting only if ALL are true:

- **(a)** It's a real, specific posting you opened or saw with its own URL (or, for board listings marked employer-reviewed with an active apply flag, the employer URL the board gives).
- **(b)** It appears open: no "closed"/"inactive"/"no longer accepting" notice, and no start date or deadline already past.
- **(c)** It's an internship, co-op, apprenticeship, placement or entry-level role (0–2 years). Reject roles requiring 3+ years, and senior, lead, manager, director or freelance senior roles.
- **(d)** It's in scope and design is the main job. Skip UX research roles unless they welcome design majors.
- **(e)** The student can plausibly be eligible. Skip roles limited to students at schools in a region that excludes Illinois, roles requiring a Master's, and roles requiring local work authorization outside the US.
- **(f)** It isn't already on the board. Same company and essentially the same title and term is a duplicate.

Roles anywhere in the world are fine. Skip unpaid roles if the posting says unpaid. Quality over volume; 0 new postings is a fine result; cap at 30 per run.

## Field rules

- **`programs`:** use the scope lists. Footwear, CMF, retail store design, model making, medical device and transportation design are ID. Apparel graphics, merchandise graphics, art direction, motion, illustration, wayfinding and production artist are GD. "Product design" means ID at physical-product companies and UX at software companies; decide from the work described. List more than one program only when the posting is explicitly open to those majors or has separate tracks.
- **`level`:** "Internship", "Co-op" or "Entry-level".
- **`term`:** e.g. "Summer 2027", "Six months from March 2027", "Full-time"; "" if unknown.
- **`location`:** city, state or country, and on-site/hybrid/remote as stated.
- **`region`:** "Chicago area", "Midwest", "Remote", "Elsewhere in US" or "International". Midwest = IL outside Chicagoland, IN, WI, MI, OH, MN, IA, MO, KS, NE, ND, SD. International = anywhere outside the US, including Canada.
- **`pay`:** as stated, with currency. "Paid (rate not listed)" if it says paid without a figure. "" if not stated. Never estimate.
- **`deadline`:** YYYY-MM-DD if stated, else "".
- **`note`:** one short plain sentence a student would find useful: specialty area, eligibility, how to apply if by email, posting language if not English. Copy facts only from the posting.
- **`facts`:** key details of the posting in your own short words (duties, requirements, how to apply). Never paste the posting's text.
- **`source`:** where you found it, e.g. "Built In Chicago" or "Watchlist · <name>".

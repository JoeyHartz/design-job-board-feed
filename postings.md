## About this list

This list is updated every weekday. Jobs are found on company career sites and design job boards, checked against the board's criteria (design is the main job; internship, co-op or 0–2 years of experience), and approved by a person before they appear. Jobs are removed when their deadline passes or the posting closes.

Questions or a job to suggest? Contact the School of Art & Design professional preparation office.

Regions: Chicago area, Midwest, Remote, Elsewhere in US, International. "Midwest" means Illinois outside Chicagoland and nearby states; "International" includes Canada.
